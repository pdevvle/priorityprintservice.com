# Pricing matrix — captured reference

**Captured 2026-08-01** from the live calculator UIs. Regenerate with
`matrix.mjs` (scratchpad harness) whenever a pricing knob moves.

## What this is, and what it is for

Every figure below was read out of a calculator's own quantity-pricing table after
driving its real controls — not computed from the constants. That distinction is the
point: it records **what a customer is quoted**, so it stays honest even if the engine
and the documented constants ever disagree.

Two uses:

1. **Reference.** What does a given product cost at a given size, paper and quantity.
2. **Regression gate.** Before restyling or porting the other calculators, re-run the
   sweep and diff. Anything that moves is either an intended change or a bug, and you
   know which. This is also phase 0 of `docs/SERVER_PRICING_SPIKE.md` — the golden
   fixture the PHP port must reproduce to the cent.

The machine-readable form is `docs/pricing-matrix.json` (all 1,816 points). The tables
here are a readable extract, not the whole set.

## Method and its limits

- Each page render yields the whole quantity ladder at once, which is what makes a
  sweep this wide tractable.
- Three axes per calculator: **every size** at default paper, **every paper** at
  default size, and **every page count** where the calculator exposes one as a select.
  Combinations *across* axes are not swept — this is a reference, not a full
  cross-product.
- Perfect bound and coupon book take page count through a number field with an Apply
  button rather than a select, so their page-count axis is not captured here. Their
  figures are all at the default page count.
- Prices exclude shipping, rush and the sale discount, which are applied after the
  calculator subtotal.
- Validated on capture: no empty or non-positive cells, and unit price falls
  monotonically with quantity on all 1,816 points.

## Surcharge state at capture

Booklet family carried `*_surcharge: 0.40` (raised from 0.30 on 2026-08-01). The five
flat calculators carry no surcharge term. See "Pricing surcharge (booklet family)" in
`MASTER_PRICING_LOGIC.md`.
